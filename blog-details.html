How a faster website increases your sales
Your website has a few seconds to make a good impression. When pages load slowly, visitors lose patience, bounce back to search results and choose a competitor instead. Speed is not just a technical detail: it directly affects how many visitors become customers.
Google uses Core Web Vitals, a set of real-world speed and stability measurements, as part of its ranking signals. A faster site therefore does double duty: it ranks better, bringing in more visitors, and it converts more of those visitors once they arrive.
Why speed matters
The good news is that most slow websites suffer from the same handful of problems, and fixing them rarely requires a full rebuild.
  • Compress and resize images, and serve modern formats like WebP.
  • Remove unused plugins, scripts and tracking tags.
  • Use a CDN and caching so pages are served close to your visitors.
  • Load non-essential content only when it is needed.
 If your site still feels slow after these fixes, a performance audit will pinpoint exactly what is holding it back. Get in touch and the FignGig team will review your website for free.
Quick wins you can apply today
Start with the changes above, measure the result with Google PageSpeed Insights, and repeat until your key pages load in under two seconds.
Speed also shapes how people feel about your brand. A site that responds instantly feels professional and trustworthy, while a sluggish one makes visitors question whether the business behind it is just as slow.
